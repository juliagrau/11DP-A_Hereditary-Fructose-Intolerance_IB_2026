**What is Fructose?**
Fructose is a Monosaccharide found in daily-consumed foods such as fruits, sweeteners, vegetables. Normally consumed but also produced in small amounts by the brain.

**What is Hereditary Fructose Intolerance?**
HFI is an autosomal recessive disorder provoked by mutations in the isoenzyme Aldolase B. When people with this disorder ingest fructose, there’s accumulations of fructose 1-phosphate and a depletion of ATP. These two consequences lead to abdominal pain, vomit, nausea, hypoglycemia (a drop of blood sugar level) and even kidney and liver failure, which can be life-threatening.

**What function does Aldolase B do?**
Aldolase B is one of the three isoenzymes needed to catalyze the reversible conversion of fructose-1,6-bisphosphate to glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate and dihydroxyacetone phosphate.
